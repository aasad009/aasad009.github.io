Evolutionary biomechanics · fossil function

Reconstructing the missing mechanics of vertebrate evolution.

Fossils preserve bones, but not the full muscles and mechanical conditions that enabled animals to move. Two linked questions ask what missing muscles can be reconstructed and which joint-function conclusions survive uncertainty. A third, complementary project tests the mechanics of vertebral regionalization.

The researcher behind the questions

Connecting computational mechanics with evolutionary discovery.

I’m a PhD candidate in mechanical engineering at UC Riverside. My published work addresses experimental design, mechanics-constrained inverse modeling, and material identifiability. These are proposed applications that would require collaboration with specialists in fossil anatomy, comparative morphology, and living-animal biomechanics.
