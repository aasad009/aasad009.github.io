Three directions in evolutionary mechanics.

A connected research program exploring how tissue mechanics shape movement, how external structures could redistribute spinal loads, and how changes in microanatomy might translate into mechanical evolution.

Research direction 01

From Tissue Responses to Joint Mobility

Select mechanical-response points from tissue-specific PCA atlases. The combined properties of skin, capsule, and muscles determine a modeled joint-mobility envelope.

Research direction 02

Sitting Upright Is Not Enough!

Explore a gait-based approach to spinal load redistribution: compare unsupported walking with an external support force path, plus standing and sitting references.

Research direction 03

From Microstructural to Mechanical Evolution

Translate a sequence of known microstructural states through one candidate transform into predicted macroscopic response fingerprints and their PCA trajectory.

Scientific scope: These are interactive research proposals, not completed experimental studies. They contain synthetic anatomy or material assumptions, illustrative priors, and reduced-order mechanical models. They do not establish validated predictions of animal mobility, human spinal offloading, or evolutionary change.
