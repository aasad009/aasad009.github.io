# Publication-to-proposal framing (updated October 2026)

This website version uses the corrected portrait with pixel sunglasses and a rainforest header, plus an explicitly proposal-connected research-methods section.

## Verified published contributions and proposed transfers

1. **Asadi & Laksari, JMBBM 2025 (OED).** Established information-aware choices of hyperelastic loading states in noise-contaminated synthetic tests. **Proposed—not demonstrated—transfer:** choose informative extant animal measurements for evaluating uncertainty in muscle paths and joint-mechanics inference.
2. **Asadi & Laksari, bioRxiv 2026 (compatibility atlas).** Established a method of reporting multiple constitutive family matches to limited data and distinguishing them by additional deformation-mode evidence on a rubber benchmark. **Proposed transfer:** joint mobility predictions under full sets of plausible tissue-response laws rather than one arbitrary fossil law.
3. **Asadi & Laksari, Annals of Biomedical Engineering 2026 (PI-UNet).** Demonstrated recovering heterogeneous nonlinear constitutive coefficients on controlled synthetic 3D finite-element datasets. **Proposed transfer:** estimate extant-species tissue properties when measured full-field deformation and relevant experimental constraints exist, then quantify uncertainty entering fossil functional comparisons. This is neither a direct muscle-reconstruction method nor a method validated on fossils.

## Scientific guardrails

- No published benchmark is presented as an extinct-animal reconstruction.
- Published/peer-reviewed works are distinguished from the bioRxiv preprint.
- Proposed translations need extant-animal comparative and physical validation and likely more sophisticated material models.
- Internal links carry the visitor directly from each established method to its proposed evolutionary use.
