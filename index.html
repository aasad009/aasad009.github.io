Research themes

My work focuses on a central question: how can deformation measurements be converted into reliable, physically interpretable maps of nonlinear tissue mechanics?

01 · Identifiability

Optimal experimental design

Quantifying the information content of loading conditions and selecting deformations that improve multiparameter hyperelastic identifiability.

Explore hyperelastic material characterization →
02 · Inverse imaging

Physics-informed hyperelastic reconstruction

Recovering voxel-wise nonlinear material parameters from volumetric deformation fields while enforcing equilibrium and boundary reactions.

Explore physics-informed inverse elastography →
03 · Ongoing

Constitutive compatibility atlases

Building noise-aware maps of where different constitutive models are distinguishable, compatible, or observationally equivalent.

About

I am Amirreza “Amir” Asadi (امیررضا اسدی), a PhD candidate in Mechanical Engineering at UC Riverside, working at the intersection of computational mechanics, medical imaging, inverse problems, and machine learning.

Research direction

My long-term goal is to develop quantitative, mechanics-based imaging methods that move beyond simplified stiffness estimates and recover spatially resolved nonlinear constitutive information.
